# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Open UI contributors and maintainers are committed to a welcoming, professional and harassment-free project community for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level or personal characteristics.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful and constructive. Discuss technical ideas and decisions rather than attacking people. Give actionable feedback, assume reasonable intent, respect boundaries, protect private information, and help keep project spaces useful for contributors at different experience levels.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, sustained disruption, publishing private information without permission, or other conduct that would reasonably make participation unsafe is not acceptable.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, review threads, project community spaces and when representing the project publicly.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate this policy and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary. Serious safety concerns should be reported privately to the maintainers rather than escalated publicly.

Maintainers should handle reports as confidentially and consistently as practical and should avoid retaliation against good-faith reporters.
